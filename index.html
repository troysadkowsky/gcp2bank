Coherence Monitor Bank

Every reading in this bank is fetched live from a 79.5-million-row MySQL table, batch by batch, and played back like a bank of heart-rate monitors. Push the speed past what the server can deliver and the sweep will tell you honestly.

The accompanying cumulative graphs visualize how synchronized attention structurally alters the baseline randomness of the physical network.

Understanding network coherence
  • The metric. Coherence measures “excess correlation” across a global grid of random number generators (RNGs). When the network deviates from standard probability, it suggests that shared human focus or emotional resonance is physically impacting the hardware’s expected chaos.
  • The running total. The cumulative graph plots these continuous deviations as a “random walk.” A flat, horizontal trend indicates baseline randomness. Steep climbs or dives represent sustained structural anomalies in the data.
Upward trends — excess variance
  • The unbounded ceiling. The data features positive spikes reaching as high as roughly +90 in a single second. Excess correlation is mathematically unconstrained, allowing the network to register extraordinarily intense, highly improbable deviations.
  • Global synchronization. A sharp upward slope on the graph indicates a sudden, massive alignment of the RNGs. This synchronization maps to moments of intense, shared global focus — often triggered by breaking news, widespread shock, or significant planetary events that capture collective attention simultaneously.
Downward trends — suppressed variance
  • The mathematical floor. The data is structurally bounded by a hard floor of −1.0, exact in every reading across all 19 clusters. Because the underlying variance measure cannot go negative, the coherence metric reaches an absolute limit when normal random “noise” is entirely flattened.
  • Collective calm or disconnection. A sustained downward slope occurs when the global network produces unnaturally smooth data. This either reflects a period of profound, internalized collective peace (such as synchronized global meditation) or a highly fractured, dispersed attention field where disconnected, localized human experiences effectively cancel each other out.
The asymmetry of global events

Because suppressed variance is capped at −1.0, downward trends require long, persistent periods of calm or dispersed energy to visibly pull the cumulative total downward. In contrast, the unconstrained nature of excess correlation means a single, unified moment of global shock can spike the data by around 90 in one second — instantly erasing days of accumulated mathematical quiet.
